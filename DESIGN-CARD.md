# Design card

The supplied wordmark artwork carries the business name exactly. Cinzel echoes its classical letterforms in page titles and oversized statements, Big Shoulders Text carries prices and labels, and Red Hat Text carries body copy. Shop wall `#F1F0EC` is the main ground, chalkboard `#1E2421` is the dark ground, logo gold `#C79B4E` and pale gold `#E7CD92` carry actions and strands, cape grey `#8B8F94` carries quiet detail, ink `#171515` carries wall text, and leather `#2A1F18` adds warmth to dark feature areas.

The home hero is a dark, full height portrait composition with the exact gold wordmark, a large ghosted icon, and a diagonal gold blade of color. Three real service strands cross into one braid as Home moves from Fades to Braids to Locs. Photography is treated as an editorial collage rather than a card grid. A fine stepped gold fade under the nav tracks progress. The icon stands alone on mobile, the lockup identifies the desktop nav, and the wordmark closes the footer.
